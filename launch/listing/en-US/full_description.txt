Flying Parcel puts you at the controls of a delivery drone with the order hanging on a rope beneath it. Lift off from the shop, fly across an open city, fight the wind and lower the parcel gently to your customer.

THE PHONE RINGS, THE ORDER IS ON
Every order starts with a call from a customer with a name. Tara, Bill, Louise and the rest of the neighborhood call in, and each order goes to the customer's own place: a rooftop, a balcony, a boat, a desert crag, a mountain chalet or an igloo.

PHYSICS THAT FEELS REAL
The parcel swings, the rope can snag and the wind keeps changing. Pizza, falafel, kunafa, coffee, cake, flowers, medicine, a TV, even a glass vase: every order has its own weight and fragility, and some cool down or melt on the way.

80 HANDCRAFTED MISSIONS
Earn up to three stars on every mission across the city, the beach, the desert, a pine forest, the mountains and the snowy peaks. Challenge missions twist the rules: a blackout, a storm front, an avalanche at your back, thin ice, a moving ferry and a very long rope.

12 AIRCRAFT TO COLLECT
Start with the Courier and spend your delivery money on racers, heavy lifters, a cinewhoop, a tiltrotor and more. Upgrade motors, shell, battery and frame in the shop.

DANGER IN THE SKY
Hawks and eagles hunt your drone, a rival drone races you to the customer, and every crash costs health. Rain, snow, fog, low clouds and sandstorms change the look of every flight.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
- Rush Hour: an endless run where every delivery buys you more time.
- Today's Mission: a fresh challenge every day, plus a weekly gift calendar.
- High Score: join the world board with a nickname and climb the ranks.
- A short training flight teaches every control.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
Play in portrait or landscape with a touch joystick and buttons you can resize. Five camera angles, a full day and night cycle and upbeat music. Missions play offline; the internet is used only for the world High Score board.

Game language: English.
