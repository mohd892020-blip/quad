# App icon source

The launcher and store icon (`icon-1024.png`, `icon-512.png`, `../res/mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png`) is a retouched composite of two photographs, both free for commercial use and modification without attribution:

- Drone and its wrapped package: U.S. Army National Guard photo by Sgt. 1st Class Brittany Conley (DVIDS ID 250802-Z-SE118-7586), public domain as a work of the US federal government.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:3D_Printed_Precision-_U_S_Army_Leverages_FPV_and_Delivery_Drones_During_Agile_Spirit_25_(9234078).jpg
- Dusk sky and coast: "Drone over the water and rocks" by Alexej Bowdurez (Unsplash), CC0 1.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drone_over_the_water_and_rocks_(Unsplash).jpg

Edits: drone cut out of its sky, placed on the dusk sky, relit warm, square crop, colour grade, grain, vignette. No text or logos.
The game must never suggest endorsement by the US Army or the Department of Defense (a condition of using DVIDS imagery).
